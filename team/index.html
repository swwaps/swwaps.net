The team

Calapya

Our open, competitive regional dragon boat team: 20 paddlers, a drummer, and a steersperson moving as one. We practice twice a week at Riverplace Marina in central Portland — and we always have room in the boat.

How to join Email the coaches →
Practices Sundays 8:30–10 am
Wednesdays 7:30–9 pm Weather permitting, on the water in a dragon boat.
Where Riverplace Marina 1510 S Harbor Way, Portland, OR 97201
Who Ages 14+, all levels Attend as many practices as you want with membership.

What practice looks like

On-water sessions run 60–90 minutes: technique drills and race pieces, with interval, endurance, and power training mixed in. All of it in the boat, all of it together.

  • Life jackets and paddles are provided
  • Wear quick-drying clothes and shoes that can get wet
  • Bring water, sunscreen, and a brimmed hat on sunny days
  • Race-team seats are earned through practice attendance
