Board of directors

The people steering the organization

Every board member is a paddler first — volunteers committed to transparency, inclusion, and getting more people out on the water.

headshot
President

Christine Landry

Founding President of SWWAPS, Christine brings over 25 years of experience building businesses and 15 years volunteering in leadership roles across education, healthcare, and community nonprofits. A water-sports veteran of 20+ years focused on Ridgefield dragon boating since early 2025, she's committed to building an inclusive, high-performing paddling community and developing its next generation of leaders.

headshot
Vice President

Ari Thornton

Ari works alongside the President and board to guide the team's vision, support the coaches, and keep the environment positive, inclusive, and competitive. Dragon boating taught them the value of discipline, communication, and unity — principles they bring to every board decision, on and off the water.

headshot
Treasurer

Christie Timm

Calapya's first Treasurer, Christie brings nine years of professional nonprofit bookkeeping and a mission-first mindset to keeping the team's finances clear and transparent. She joined two years ago as a paddler looking for a fun way to stay active — and found a community built on teamwork and plenty of laughter.

headshot
Secretary

Anna Hernandez

Anna keeps SWWAPS records organized, accurate, and well-documented in service of the board's transparency goals. Originally from Alaska, she brings a genuine respect for the outdoors and a personal mission to give back — including hand-delivering more than 250 care packages to cancer patients over multiple holiday seasons. She's now in her second season paddling with Calapya.

headshot
Steering Committee Chair

Jen Sedwick

Jen caught the dragon boating bug in 2022 and hasn't looked back — the sport is now a centerpiece of her life and social circle (she even got her husband in the boat). Off the water she's a veteran product designer with two decades of experience, and an avid hiker, biker, and all-around paddle-sports enthusiast.

Volunteerism is the whole engine

SWWAPS runs on members and community volunteers. If you'd like to help — on the board, at events, or behind the scenes — we'd love to hear from you.
