Sponsors & support

Help us put more paddles in the water

SWWAPS is a nonprofit. Sponsorships and donations keep dues low, fund boats and safety equipment, and open the sport to anyone in southwest Washington who wants to try it.

What sponsorship does

Access

Keeps season dues low and first practices free, so cost never keeps anyone off the water.

Equipment

Funds paddles, life jackets, and boat maintenance — the gear we hand every new paddler.

Visibility

Your name travels with the team — at four Northwest race festivals and community events all season.
