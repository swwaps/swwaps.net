Never held a paddle? Perfect.

No experience, no gear, no commitment. Anyone 14 and up can hop in the boat — we teach you everything.

01 — Show up

Come to any practice in clothes you don't mind getting wet. Life jackets and paddles are provided.

02 — Paddle free, three times

Your first three practices cost nothing. Fill out the waiver, email the coaches, and we'll get you on the boat.

03 — Join Calapya

Hooked? Season dues are $230, with as many practices as you want and the option to race.

Meet the team

Calapya

Calapya is our open, competitive dragon boat team: 20 paddlers, a drummer, and a steersperson moving as one. On the water we train technique, endurance, and power. Off the water we hike, barbecue, and celebrate birthdays — the boat comes with friends built in.

A nonprofit for paddling in southwest Washington

SWWAPS exists to make paddling sports safe, inclusive, and easy to get into — through education, community events, and recreational and competitive opportunities.

  • Encourage recreational and competitive paddling
  • Support paddling education and safety awareness
  • Promote stewardship of local waterways
  • Partner with the community to expand access
